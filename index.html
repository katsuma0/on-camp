About

on-camp is a private journal for Ontario Parks campsites. Walk a campground, rate each site out of five, add a note or a photo, and mark the ones worth booking again. It covers every reservable Ontario Park, down to the campgrounds, the individual sites, and the trails.

I book the camping trips for my friends and family, and choosing a site you have never seen is a gamble. Some are good and some are not, and a year later you cannot remember which was which. This is the notebook I wanted for that.

Everything you save stays on this device. There is no account, no server, and nothing is tracked. Ratings and notes live in the browser and photos in local storage, and you can export a backup to a file and load it on another phone. Please do not photograph occupied sites, and leave a site the way you would want to find it.
